# June 18 Vintages Preview: Burgundy and Patagonia

This week's Special Offers release is a relatively small one, featuring two producers that may not be familiar to every Ontario collector:

* Domaine des Petits Champs Lins (Burgundy)
* Bodega Chacra (Patagonia)

There are no First Growths, Super Tuscans, or major trophy bottles in this release. Still, there are several wines that collectors may find worth exploring.

## Bodega Chacra

Bodega Chacra was founded by Piero Incisa della Rocchetta, grandson of Mario Incisa della Rocchetta, the creator of Sassicaia at Tenuta San Guido.

The estate focuses on Pinot Noir and Chardonnay from Patagonia, where cool temperatures and large day-night temperature swings help preserve freshness and acidity.

### Chacra Treinta y Dos Pinot Noir 2024

98 JS · $199

Named after the vineyard's 1932 planting date, Treinta y Dos is produced from some of the oldest Pinot Noir vines in Patagonia.

Expect a more restrained style than many New World Pinots, with red cherry, rose petal, dried herbs and mineral notes rather than overt ripeness.

For collectors interested in producer pedigree and site expression, this is arguably the most interesting bottle in the release.

### Chacra Chardonnay 2024

98 JS · $164

Chacra's Chardonnay has developed a strong following among collectors looking for alternatives to increasingly expensive white Burgundy.

The style tends to emphasize citrus, orchard fruit, saline minerality and precision rather than richness.

## Domaine des Petits Champs Lins

This producer makes its LCBO debut with a range of Burgundy appellations spanning village, premier cru and grand cru sites.

### Clos des Fiètres Corton Grand Cru 2022

$175

Grand Cru Burgundy at this price point is becoming increasingly uncommon.

The 2022 vintage was warm and generous, producing wines with concentration and structure while maintaining freshness.

Collectors looking for a bottle to cellar may find this one particularly interesting.

### Puligny-Montrachet 2023

$187

Puligny-Montrachet remains one of Burgundy's benchmark white wine villages.

Expect citrus, stone fruit, white flowers and mineral character, with the balance and tension that have made the appellation famous.

### Hospices de Beaune Savigny-Lès-Beaune 2023

$205

The Hospices de Beaune has been one of Burgundy's most important institutions since the 15th century.

Wines connected to the Hospices often attract attention from Burgundy enthusiasts because of their provenance and historical significance.

## Final Thoughts

This is not a headline-grabbing release, but there are several wines worth a closer look.

The Chacra wines provide an opportunity to explore one of Patagonia's most respected estates, while the Burgundy offerings include a Grand Cru and a Hospices bottling that may appeal to collectors seeking something a little different from the usual names.
